Article 14 guarantees Right to Equality before law.

Article 19 guarantees freedoms such as speech and expression, movement, and profession.

Article 21 guarantees Right to Life and Personal Liberty.

Article 32 allows citizens to move the Supreme Court for enforcement of fundamental rights.

The 42nd Amendment is known as the Mini Constitution and made significant changes to the Indian Constitution in 1976.

The 44th Amendment restored civil liberties and made changes after Emergency period.

Fundamental Duties are listed under Article 51A of the Constitution.
